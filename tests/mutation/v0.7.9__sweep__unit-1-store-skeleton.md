## What the sweep taught

- **M09 is the interesting one.** The guard looked like input validation; it is actually what keeps a swallowed exception from throwing away a raw row. `archive` catching everything is right (the store is additive), and it makes every guard inside the projection a guard on the *raw* row's survival too.
- The `INSERT OR IGNORE` on sources (M05) was killed by the idempotence test's source count, not by anything asserting `first_seen` -- adequate for now; the resume-position work in #22 step 4 will need a `first_seen` assertion of its own.

## Caveats

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing -- and **a clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness.** No mutant touched `_LINKS`, the orphan query, or the backup file naming; those are covered by direct tests but not by this sweep.
