## What the sweep taught

- **A clean first round was the signal to go again, and the second round found three.** The first generator attacked guards, which the red-green audit had already proved attached; the second, told what had been tried, attacked *what is stored* -- and two of the three survivors were columns no test had read.
- **`sources.locator` was written and never asserted.** The row existed, the test counted it, nothing looked inside. The same class as the fingerprint's "varied two members at once": a count is not a contract.

## Caveats

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing -- and **a clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness.** The two-round cap was reached; the `status_json` path and `capi_shipyard` have no mutant of their own.
