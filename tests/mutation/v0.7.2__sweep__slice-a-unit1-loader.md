## Caveats

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing — and **a clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness.** The red-green audit and this sweep disagreed nowhere this time; the audit's six mutants were all killed by the tests written for them, and the sweep's twelve found five gaps the audit's hand-picked set could not, which is the reason both run.
