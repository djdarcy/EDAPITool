## Caveats

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing — and **a clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness.** The audit and the sweep disagreed nowhere; the sweep found two gaps the audit's eight hand-picked reverts could not, and one of them — column A — is the kind the probe's author was blind to precisely because the probe's cases were written from the real workbook's columns, none of which is A.
