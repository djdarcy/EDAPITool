## What the sweep taught

- **The red-green audit and the sweep disagreed, and the disagreement was the information.** The audit's anchor (`test_two_stations_with_no_market_fingerprint_differently`) is a true anchor -- it fails under the old grid hash -- and still let six single-member mutants through, because it varied two members at once. Anchors prove attachment, not tightness.
- **Text-mode fixtures on Windows are CRLF fixtures.** `write_text` and `open(..., "a")` both translate, so a whole test file believed itself to be testing LF. Any test that means LF must write bytes.
- **Fleet carriers with no orders are a market.** M02 was the generator's guess; EDMC's `eddn.py` confirms it sends the empty commodity list deliberately. The empty-market fence is the store's concern too: an empty `market_snapshot` must be a valid row.

## Caveats

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing -- and **a clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness.** The wiring test (M08) is structural and is labelled so; it guards one line by its text, not by its effect.
